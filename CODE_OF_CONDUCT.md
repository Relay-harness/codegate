# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and publication of another person's private information are not welcome. Assume good intent when possible, and keep feedback about the work rather than the person.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to repository owners through GitHub; do not include private information in a public issue. We will handle reports as fairly and confidentially as practical, but cannot promise a response time.
